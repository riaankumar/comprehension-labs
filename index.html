Comprehension Labs

Human progress depends on our ability to pass understanding from one mind to another. We believe AI can extend that ability—making patient, thoughtful teaching available to anyone.

That future requires models that understand how people learn: where an idea breaks down, why a mistake makes sense, and what helps someone see a problem differently. Our mission is to build the data that makes this possible.

We are starting with education. We work with universities and assessment providers to turn real learning processes into verified data for training and evaluating frontier models.

A final answer captures a moment. The work around it tells a richer story: the first attempt, the misconception, the instructor’s feedback, the revision, and the eventual outcome. We bring those pieces together so models can learn from the path to understanding.

  1. Capture the process. We reconstruct complete records of educational work through institutional partnerships, with consent and commercial rights established from the start.
  2. Ground it in expertise. Qualified reviewers examine the work and its outcomes, giving each record the context needed to make it useful.
  3. Test what it improves. We build around a specific model failure and evaluate the data against your acceptance criteria before delivery.

Every dataset comes with a clear record of its source, permissions, and review. Each buyer’s data is kept separate.

Education is our starting point because it brings a fundamental challenge into focus: producing an answer is only one part of intelligence. Recognizing confusion, adapting an explanation, and helping someone reason for themselves are capabilities worth building toward.

We begin with a paid 4–6 week pilot focused on one workflow and a measurable goal.
